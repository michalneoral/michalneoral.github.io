{% include image.html url="/images/profile.jpg" width=300 align="right" %} My name is Michal Neoral and I am a researcher at the Visual Recognition Group (VRG) at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering, Czech Technical University in Prague. I completed my Ph.D. in 2025 under the supervision of prof. Jiří Matas and Dr. Jan Šochman. My dissertation, Dense Motion Estimation in a Monocular Video, covers dense pixel-level tracking in video, with emphasis on optical flow estimation, occlusion handling, and motion segmentation. My broader research interests lie in long-term correspondence estimation, where I aim to develop robust methods that maintain temporal consistency across extended video sequences. More recently I have been working on detection, classification and fine-grained recognition for fast processing of large-scale data, in the AISee, Median, CEDMO, FunDive and Local CLS projects. During my Ph.D. I had the opportunity to collaborate with Toyota Motor Europe, and I would like to sincerely thank them for their generous support throughout my doctoral studies.
